Add a `borderType` option to Google Pay, passed to Google's [`buttonBorderType`](https://developers.google.com/pay/api/web/reference/request-objects#ButtonOptions). `"no_border"` removes the thin border Google draws around the button; `"default_border"` (the default) keeps it.
